## Maintenance Cadence

- Weekly: review Issues/PRs and update `CHANGELOG.md`.
- Every 1-2 weeks: ship one focused release.
- Monthly: refresh screenshots, examples, topics, and compatibility notes.

## Launch Channels

- `show-hn.md`
- `product-hunt.md`
- `x-thread.md`
- `reddit-post.md`
- `devto-article-outline.md`
