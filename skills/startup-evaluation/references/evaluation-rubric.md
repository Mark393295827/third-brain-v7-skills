# Startup Evaluation Rubric

Load this reference only when a numeric score, financing diagnosis, or investor memo is required.

## Evidence Ladder

| Level | Evidence | Interpretation |
|---:|---|---|
| 0 | Founder belief, market slide, friendly feedback | Very weak |
| 1 | Interviews about past behavior | Weak |
| 2 | Landing page, waitlist, demo usage | Moderate |
| 3 | Payment, repeat usage, signed pilot | Strong |
| 4 | Retention, expansion, referral, healthy economics | Very strong |

## Weighted Score

Score each dimension 0-5, multiply by weight, and normalize to 100. Adjust weights only with a stated stage/type reason.

| Dimension | Weight | Healthy evidence | Red flag |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Customer pain and beachhead | 15 | Narrow urgent workflow, reachable buyer | Vague user, nice-to-have |
| Market and timing | 15 | Focused profitable niche or scalable market, timing window | Market-size-only logic |
| Value proposition | 15 | Step-change value, cost, or capability | Marginal improvement |
| PMF and traction | 15 | Payment, retention, pull, repeatable channel | Paid push, churn, weak use |
| Business model and economics | 10 | Clear pricing, margin path, LTV/CAC >3 at scale | Unknown CAC, long payback |
| Team and governance | 15 | Founder-market fit, complementary roles, decision rules | Role gaps, conflict |
| Capital and runway | 10 | Milestone-based spend, financing plan | Crisis fundraising, vanity burn |
| Moat and risk control | 5 | Data, network, workflow, distribution, trust, regulation | Easily copied dependency |

Scale: `0 absent`, `1 narrative`, `2 weak signal`, `3 plausible`, `4 evidence-backed`, `5 strong and repeatable`.

Interpretation: `80-100 healthy`, `65-79 promising`, `50-64 fragile`, `0-49 redesign/pause`. The label never overrides a fatal risk.

## Investor 5T

| Lens | Question |
|---|---|
| Team | Why this team; what execution proof exists? |
| Target Market | Can this produce a power-law return? |
| Tech/Product | What is defensible beyond current components? |
| Traction | Is growth pulled by retention and customers? |
| Terms | Do valuation, dilution, and structure permit returns? |

## Financing Signals

| Metric | Green | Yellow | Red |
|---|---|---|---|
| Runway | 18+ months | 6-18 months | Under 6 months |
| Fundraising start | Before 12 months remain | 6-12 months | Crisis already started |
| LTV/CAC | 3-5+ | 1-3 | Under 1 or unknown at scale |
| CAC payback | Under 12 months | 12-18 months | Over 18 months |
| Burn | Tied to next milestone | Mixed | Vanity spend |

Capital should reduce the top uncertainty or cross a named milestone.
